PERFUME TYPES

Discover the different types of fragrances categorized by concentration and longevity. Understanding each type helps you choose the right scent for your lifestyle, preferences, and occasions. From light and refreshing mists to rich and long-lasting parfums, explore the world of fragrance and find your perfect match.

Parfum, also known as Extrait, has the highest fragrance concentration among all types. It is very rich and intense, usually lasting more than 8 hours on the skin. Because of its strength, only a small amount is needed for all-day wear.
Eau de Parfum has a high concentration of fragrance oils, making it strong and long-lasting. It typically stays on the skin for about 6-8 hours. This type is ideal for evening wear or special occasions.
Eau de Toilette is lighter and fresher compared to EDP. It usually lasts around 3-5 hours, making it suitable for daily or casual use. It gives a noticeable scent without being too overpowering.
Eau de Cologne has a lower fragrance concentration, making it light and refreshing. It typically lasts 2-3 hours on the skin. This type is perfect for hot weather or quick reapplication throughout the day.
Body mists have the lightest concentration among all fragrance types. They provide a subtle scent that usually lasts 1-2 hours. They are ideal for a quick refresh or layering with other fragrances.

SCENT FAMILIES

Explore the world of fragrance through its main scent families and subfamilies. Each family represents a unique personality from fresh and vibrant to warm and sensual while subfamilies reveal the subtle variations within them.

Floral fragrances are inspired by flowers and are often soft, romantic, and elegant.

  • Single Floral (Soliflore) - Focuses on one dominant flower (e.g., rose or jasmine).
  • Floral Bouquet - A blend of multiple floral notes.
  • Floral Fruity - Floral base mixed with sweet fruit notes.
  • Floral Green - Floral scents with fresh, leafy accents.
  • Floral Oriental - Florals blended with warm spices or amber.

Fresh fragrances are light, clean, and energizing.

  • Citrus - Lemon, bergamot, orange, grapefruit.
  • Aquatic (Marine) - Ocean breeze, watery, airy notes.
  • Green - Grass, leaves, herbs.
  • Aromatic - Lavender, rosemary, sage (often used in men's scents).

Woody fragrances are warm, earthy, and grounding.

  • Woody Dry - Cedarwood, vetiver, sandalwood.
  • Woody Mossy (Chypre) - Woody base with oakmoss and citrus.
  • Woody Spicy - Woods blended with pepper or spices.
  • Woody Oriental - Woods mixed with amber and vanilla.

Oriental fragrances are rich, warm, and sensual.

  • Soft Oriental - Amber mixed with light florals.
  • Oriental Spicy - Cinnamon, clove, cardamom.
  • Oriental Woody - Deep woods with amber warmth.
  • Oriental Vanilla (Gourmand) - Sweet, dessert-like warmth.

Gourmand scents are inspired by edible notes and sweet treats.

  • Vanilla-Based - Creamy, sweet vanilla core.
  • Chocolate/Coffee - Rich, roasted, dessert-like.
  • Caramel/Sugary - Warm, candy-like sweetness.
